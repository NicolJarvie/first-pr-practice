Rune Raid: Viking Saga

The runes of your ancestors — your honour, kept in stone — have been stolen. Three thieves. Three lands. Every rune gone by dawn. Gather your warband and take them back.

Google Play — coming soon

The game

What you'll do

Build your warband

Shield maidens, seeresses, berserkers and more. Each raider fights their own way, and recovered runes unlock new ones.

Raid the strongholds

Send your raiders down the route, past ballistas and storm towers, to break into the vault.

Three lands to reclaim

Fight your way from Nordheim to Dvergheim and Solmark, each with its own thief to face.

Free and fair

Nothing to buy, ever. Ads are optional, and you always see the reward before you choose to watch.
