Runic Raid: Viking Saga

A Viking raiding game for your phone. Build a warband, send them down the route and break into the strongholds of three lands to win back your clan's stolen runes.

Google Play — coming soon

The story

Take them back

The runes of your ancestors — your honour, kept in stone — were taken in a single night by three thieves from three lands.

Nordheim keeps its share. Dvergheim locks its share away. Sólmark sells its share to the sun. Board the longship, gather your warband and restore your clan's honour, one rune at a time.

The game

What you'll do

Build your warband

Shield maidens, seeresses, berserkers and more. Each raider fights their own way, and recovered runes unlock new ones.

Raid the strongholds

Send your raiders down the route, past ballistas and storm towers, to break into the vault.

Three lands to reclaim

Nordheim, Dvergheim and Sólmark each hide their share of the runes in their own way. Raid all three.

Free and fair

Nothing to buy, ever. Ads are optional, and you always see the reward before you choose to watch.
