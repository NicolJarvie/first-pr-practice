Fair play policy

Our promise to players

Every Jar of Life game is built around a few simple rules.

No pay-to-win

Skill and time decide who wins. Money never buys an advantage.

No loot boxes

If you buy something, you know exactly what you're getting.

No dark patterns

No fake timers, guilt-trip pop-ups or tricks to keep you tapping.

Respect for your data

We collect only what the game needs to work, and never sell it.

About

One developer, one jar of ideas

The Jar of Life is a solo studio. Every game is designed, built and supported by one person — which means every player email gets read and every design decision is made with players in mind.
