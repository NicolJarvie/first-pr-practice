Fair play policy

Our promise to players

Every Jar of Life game is built around a few simple rules.

Free, nothing to buy

No purchases of any kind. Everything in the game is earned by playing.

Ads only if you choose

No banners and no forced ads. You can choose to watch an ad for a bonus, and you see the reward before you tap.

No pay-to-win

Money can't buy an advantage, because there's nothing to buy. Skill and time decide who wins.

Respect for your data

No accounts and no sign-up. See our privacy policy for exactly what the ads collect.

About
