Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy covers the mobile games published by The Jar of Life, including Runic Raid: Viking Saga, and this website.

The short version

  • No accounts and no sign-up. We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details inside the game.
  • No in-app purchases, so we never handle payment details.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to serve those ads. Details are below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Runic Raid shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward. There are no banners and no forced ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with the ad.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's privacy policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Data we collect ourselves

We don't collect personal information through the game. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don't share them.

Children

Runic Raid is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
